# Orbital Landscapes — Collection 01

A free prototype collection illustrating a digital image-pack workflow. This is not exclusive stock photography and these are not photographs made by Small Hours or the user. The five images originate from the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) Earth As Art 5 collection. No purchase is required to access them at their original sources.

## Files and processing

Five JPEG images, each 2400 × 2400 px, plus this source record, LICENSE.txt and a JSON manifest. Full-resolution official poster ZIPs are linked below. The poster originals are 7800 × 8400 px; the pack contains a 7200 × 7200 px crop starting at x=300, y=450, resized to 2400 px. Only conventional crop/resize/JPEG encoding was performed with macOS sips. The cropped poster border contained agency identifiers and captions; credit and descriptions are retained here instead. The source's processed satellite color remains unchanged. No generative editing was used. These are remote-sensing images, not natural-color ground photography.
