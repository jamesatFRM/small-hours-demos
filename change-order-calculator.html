How much is due after a change order?

When a customer adds work after paying a deposit, two numbers matter: what would bring the deposit up to date, and what remains on the whole job. Enter the revised terms to see both.

A cabinet refinishing example

Suppose a $1,375 quote increases to $1,625 and the customer has paid $300. A 40% deposit on the revised price is $650. Another $350 would bring the deposit up to date. The projected job balance is $1,325, including that $350. Once the customer pays the $350, the balance falls to $975.
